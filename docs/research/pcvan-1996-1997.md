# PC-VAN text-service evidence, circa 1996–1997

Research for [Issue #3](https://github.com/takahirox/pcvan/issues/3), inspected
2026-10-20. The target is the text-terminal service. This reference does not
specify the Windows graphical navigator or implement an application.

## Reading the evidence

Evidence categories describe provenance, not a ranking that proves authenticity:

| Category | Kind | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Contemporary logs | Period text preserved on a later website; preservation may alter formatting. |
| B | Firsthand operator/user documentation | A participant's account; its date and scope still need checking. |
| C | Retrospective reproductions | Later transcriptions or illustrations, not authenticated period captures. |
| D | Implementation assumptions | Original demonstration or inferred behavior, never historical evidence. |

Fixture statuses are independent of categories: `verified` means the cited
source directly supports the item **within the stated scope**, not independent
authentication; `provisional` means its historical date, applicability or value
is unresolved; `reconstructed` means deliberately authored demonstration data or
behavior. A null value means unknown, never an invitation to infer a command.

  `1`–`21`, `X`, `Q`, in transcription order. All are `provisional` historical
  items from category C, even though their labels were checked against the page.
  Selector strings use ASCII; labels retain source width and punctuation.

  terminal width, colors and key acceptance rules are not specified.

## ECOM prompts are service-specific

The [1996-07-06 ECOM log](https://elestamp.o.oo7.jp/Computer_history.htm)
shows the following two prompt forms (HTML spacing normalized; the user's
following `1` is omitted):

```text
番号またはコマンド(H,Q,E,J)=
番号またはコマンド(M,S,F,H,Q,RBn,TLn,KW,KWL,DSn,RT,DLn,J)=
```

The first belongs to the ECOM service menu; the second to its guidance article
list. They are category A, `verified` observations limited to that session.
The displayed tokens, including `RBn`, `TLn`, `KW`, `KWL`, `DSn`, `RT` and `DLn`,
have no assigned meanings here. Do not generalize them to SIG forums or derive
read, search, reply, compose or navigation behavior from their spellings.
The retrospective MAIN transcription instead ends in `番号またはコマンド=`.

## Synthetic post-header demonstration

The [1996 sample](https://www.rumic.org/forum/rumicworld/rumic2491.html)
supports the field order below (category A, `verified` structure). Every value,
ID, title, forum name and body line below is original synthetic placeholder
content (category D, `reconstructed`), not an archived post or real identity:

```text
#42/9001　デモフォーラム
★タイトル (DEMO0000)  96/ 7/ 6  12:34  ( 2)
RE#41 表示確認用のサンプル
★内容
これは再現画面のために作成した例文です。
実在の投稿や利用者を表していません。
```

`42` demonstrates the post number. The meaning of the secondary number after
`/` is **unknown**; it must not be treated as a reply parent, total or sequence
without further evidence. The parenthesized field is the archived line-count
field, but its historical counting rule is **unknown** (body, title, blank lines,
wrapping, etc.). The demonstration's `2` is arbitrary, not a validated historical
count. `RE#41` illustrates a title reference only; no reply command or automatic
thread model is established. Whitespace here is illustrative, not byte-exact.

## Explicitly unresolved behavior

| Question | Status | Implementation consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which MAIN variant appeared during 1997, and when did it change? | Provisional; no dated 1997 MAIN evidence in these references. | Label the fixture circa 1996; do not promise a year-wide 1997 menu. |
| How did MAIN lead through intermediate SIG menus to DAISIZEN? | Provisional; the 1992 transcription and undated NEC screen cannot fill this gap. | No invented intermediate selectors, prompts or DAISIZEN jump keyword. |
| What was DAISIZEN's exact 1996–1997 forum configuration? | Provisional; seven-item list is undated and history records a 1992 split. | Present its provenance and uncertainty wherever reused. |
| What were SIG forum read/reply/compose prompts, submission/cancel steps and editor controls? | Provisional; not established by these sources. | A future demonstration workflow must be explicitly reconstructed (D), not attributed to ECOM or the archive. |
| Which keyboard shortcuts worked, including Enter defaults, case/width acceptance, paging and back keys? | Provisional beyond the exact observed inputs/prompts. | Do not infer meanings or global availability from prompt letters. |

## Validation and reuse

Validate both JSON files with a UTF-8 parser, including rejection of duplicate
object keys. Check unique MAIN selectors, DAISIZEN section labels/forum numbers
and MITI step inputs; require per-item status and HTTP(S) source URLs. Check MAIN's
ordered `1`–`21`, `X`, `Q`, the seven forum labels/numbers and the three MITI
inputs against this reference and their cited source. Null DAISIZEN navigation
values must remain explicit. Check relative Markdown links and staged whitespace
with `git diff --cached --check`. No application/build suite is configured or
needed for these data and documentation files.

Only functional menu labels, command tokens and structural markers are retained.
Do not bulk-copy archived posts, real people's identities or copyrighted
screenshots. Future sample messages must remain original and marked reconstructed.
